// PURPOSE & PROBLEM SOLVED

Eliminate Manual Label Typing & Misaligned Prints

Every new school academic year in Cambodia, hundreds of thousands of students and teachers struggle with creating neat, consistent book labels across multiple school subjects.

KH Book Label Maker was engineered to solve this end-to-end: simply input student details once, select your school subject bundle (Khmer Literature, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Geography, Earth Science, Foreign Language, Morality), pick an authentic Khmer traditional frame, and instantly generate an aligned A4 multi-label sheet ready for printing or PDF export.

// CAPABILITIES

Engineered For Accuracy & Cambodian Heritage

Authentic Khmer Kbach Frames

Includes a curated gallery of high-detail traditional Cambodian decorative borders and corner motifs that honor Khmer craftsmanship.

Automated A4 Print Layout

Mathematical grid positioning calculates exact margin offsets, cutting guides, and bleed lines so every sticker prints cleanly on standard sticker sheets.

School & Logo Presets

Preloaded with MoEYS standardized subjects and lets schools or classes upload custom school badges, logos, and academic years.

Khmer Unicode Typography

Flawless subscript consonants (cheung), dependent vowels, and diacritic rendering without broken ligatures or misalignment.

Lightweight MSIX Package

Installs via Microsoft Store with sandboxed security, instant clean uninstallation, and automatic background updates without bloat.

Privacy & Offline Ready

Operates 100% offline without requiring internet access, account registration, tracking cookies, or external server uploads.
