// CELEBRATING KHMER CULTURE DIGITALLY

Preserve and Showcase the Beauty of Khmer Script

The Khmer alphabet contains the longest alphabet system in the world with elaborate subscripts and ligatures. Combining these with centuries-old Angkorian ornamental motifs (Kbach) creates unparalleled cultural identity.

Khmer Frame Name Maker lets anyone type their Khmer or English name, apply artistic gradient coloring, soft glowing text shadows, and place it within authentic royal, festival (Khmer New Year, Pchum Ben), or modern aesthetic frames in seconds.

High-resolution square avatars (1:1), story cards (9:16), and banner ratios
Includes Chhuk Krapom and curated Khmer stylized calligraphic fonts
Instant 1-tap sharing to Telegram, Facebook, TikTok, and Instagram
Khmer Cultural Typography Studio
// CAPABILITIES

Artistic Features & Customization

Angkor & Modern Frames

A rich collection of gold, silver, floral, and traditional Angkorian temple frames with intricate carved detail.

Gradient & Neon Glows

Apply multi-color gradients, neon light flares, 3D embossing, and specular drop shadows to your custom name text.

Khmer Unicode Typography

Support for 20+ specialized Khmer fonts with perfect conjunct cluster rendering and zero missing glyphs.

Custom Photo Integration

Place your portrait or profile picture inside the frame alongside your name with circular or organic masking.

Lossless HD Export

Export in crystal-clear 2K/4K resolution PNG with transparency or vibrant JPG suitable for high-density phone displays.

Festive Season Bundles

Seasonal updates containing specialized graphics for Khmer New Year (Choul Chnam Thmey), Pchum Ben, and Water Festival.
