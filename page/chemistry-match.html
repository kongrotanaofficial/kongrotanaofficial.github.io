// LEARNING THROUGH GAMIFICATION

Turn Chemical Formulas into Addictive Gameplay

Memorizing chemical symbols, valence numbers, and bonding pairs has traditionally been rote and tedious for STEM students.

Chemistry Match gamifies the periodic table through tactile tile connections. Players match reactive cations and anions (such as Na⁺ + Cl⁻ → NaCl, or H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O), navigate element families (alkali metals, halogens, noble gases), and trigger combo chains that reinforce real chemical rules.

Authentic IUPAC chemical nomenclature and atomic numbers
Haptic touch feedback and dynamic particle reaction bursts
Daily challenges, endless modes, and timed arcade challenges
Interactive Formula Balancing Engine
